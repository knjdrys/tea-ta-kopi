Our story

A small neighborhood spot in Dolores, Capas, built around good drinks and a warm welcome.

Message us to order

A cup, a home, a coffee bean

Tea-Ta is short for "A Sip of Tita's Warm Embrace", and the logo carries that feeling. The barista is the welcome you get at the window. The little house on the letter A is our nod to home, the place a good drink should make you feel. And the bean in the O is the coffee at the heart of everything we pour, from a Spanish latte to a wintermelon milk tea.

Made for the afternoon pause

Tea-Ta is short for "A Sip of Tita's Warm Embrace". We opened in 2024 with a simple idea: keep the menu wide, the prices fair, and the service quick, so neighbors have a spot to feel looked after.

Everything is made to order at our window. Sit on the little benches out front, or take it with you. Either way, you are part of the neighborhood we built this for.
