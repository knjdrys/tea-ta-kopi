A Sip That Feels Like Home

Tea-Ta is built around the simple moments that make a place special. A familiar cup, a friendly welcome, and a quick stop that feels a little more personal.

The name reflects that feeling. Something warm, familiar, and close to home. From coffee to milk tea and refreshing drinks, every cup is made to be part of your everyday moments, whether it is a quiet afternoon, a quick break, or time shared with someone you love.

Because sometimes, a good drink is not just about what is in the cup. It is about how the moment makes you feel.

Tea-Ta, made for moments worth savoring.

Made for the afternoon pause

Tea-Ta is short for "A Sip of Tita's Warm Embrace". We opened in 2024 with a simple promise: a wide menu, fair prices, and quick service, so neighbors always have a place that feels looked after.

Every cup is made to order at our window. Take a seat on the benches out front, or carry it with you. Either way, you are part of the neighborhood we built this for.
