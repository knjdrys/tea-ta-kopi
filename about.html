Our story

A small place built around good drinks, fair prices, and a warm welcome.

How it started

From a little palamig store to Tea-Ta Kopi.

Tea-Ta Kopi began in 2016 as a small palamig store in Dolores, Capas. People passing by would stop for a cold drink before continuing on their way. There was no elaborate setup, just something refreshing for a hot afternoon.

Over time, we added shakes, fries, and siomai. The little store grew into a familiar neighborhood stop, one simple menu item at a time.

In 2024, that little store became Tea-Ta Kopi. We kept the easygoing spirit and added coffee, milk tea, fruit tea, and more room to sit, slow down, and enjoy a drink together.

We are still a small café in Dolores. That is the point. Whether you are picking up your usual, meeting a friend, or taking a break after class, we are happy to have you here.

The Tea-Ta we know today

Good drinks. Good company. No fuss.

We make each order fresh at the window and keep the choices clear so it is easy to find something you will like.

Most drinks are priced for an everyday stop. Our menu is wide enough for the whole group, from a quick iced coffee to a fruity Yakult or a savory siomai snack.
